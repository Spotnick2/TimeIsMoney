# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release, for World of Warcraft: Forever (Interface 16001).

Time Is Money is a single-player mini-game in an enchanted goblin ledger. It is a
faithful port of Universal Paperclips, by Frank Lantz: your company makes Handfuls
of Copper Bolts, then keeps going. Start with **/tim start**.

- **The whole game.** All three phases, 96 projects, investments, strategic
  tournaments, the planet and the stars, battles, both prestige routes and every
  ending. The numbers match the original game.
- **Company funds and materials are simulated.** The game never touches your gold,
  your bags, the Auction House or your professions.
- **The ledger window.** Cards appear as the company grows, with a "New" tag on
  what just arrived. Every button, item and card title has a tooltip. Unavailable
  actions say why and where the missing resource comes from.
- **Gazlowe, your Director.** He greets you when the ledger opens, talks when
  reports arrive, and now and then offers a deal. Click him (or the reports) for
  the report history. A 2D portrait is available in Settings.
- **Pause.** /tim pause, or the button in Settings, stops the company until you
  resume. Closing the window does not pause it: the company keeps working while you
  play.
- **Settings.** The Director's model, his voice, the report sound, the minimap
  button, the window scale and a new game.
- **Saving.** Progress is saved when you log out or /reload. A crash or forced
  close can lose progress since the last save. Nothing happens while you are
  offline.

Commands: /tim start (found your company), /tim (open or close the ledger), /tim pause, /tim settings, /tim reports,
/tim minimap, /tim help, /tim status.
